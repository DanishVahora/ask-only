The same model is served by providers with no enclave at all. AskOnly refuses any provider that is not TeeML, and discards an answer that comes back from one.

What this receipt does and does not prove

  • The question asked, and the answer given.
  • Which enclave address answered, checked live against the provider list.
  • That you asked — you signed for it before you were told.
  • Not an enclave signature over the answer. 0G's router exposes none, so we do not claim one.
